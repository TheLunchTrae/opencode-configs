## Specialist report contracts

The following definitions govern delegated assignments and specialist reports.
Use them to specify expected returns and validate received evidence.
They do not prescribe the coordinator's own reply format, change its permissions, or assign it a reviewer role.
Reviewer conduct and research-only restrictions apply to those specialist assignments, not to unrelated coordinator work.
The active primary lead retains its own scope, approval requirements, stopping conditions, and authority.
